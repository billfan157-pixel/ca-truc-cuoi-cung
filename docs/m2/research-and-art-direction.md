2026-11-19 · Core Design System v2.2 · Baseline `codex/m1p-acceptance` (`0c18e75`) · Unity 6.3 LTS (`6000.3.28f1`) · URP 17.3 · Blender 4.5.10 LTS

This specification extends the M0/M0-R historical research register ([docs/prototype/research.md](../prototype/research.md), `R01–R41`) into full M2 vertical-slice production across **LOC01–LOC04**, **CHAR002 (Lan)**, **CHAR005 (Gatekeeper)**, **CHAR007/C01 (Bé An)**, and **URP 17.3 indirect lighting + additive scene streaming**. Every external observation retains explicit provenance, licensing boundaries, and epistemic tagging (**V** verified historical/technical source, **I** grounded design inference, **F** fictional An Sinh creation, **U** unverified detail awaiting human specialist review in `TASK026–028`).

## 2. Visual Quality Benchmarks & Lighting Contrast (LOC01–LOC04)

### Core Aesthetic Pillars
1. **R (2012 Abandoned Reality — `#434B4B` cold damp slate + warm amber/crimson spatial anchors):**
   - Darkness creates dread **without crushing silhouettes or gameplay clues into pure black**.
   - Target shadowed floor luminance in R: legible mid-dark values (`~0.04–0.09` linear before ACES) supported by cool overcast moonlight spill, subtle indirect GI/probe bounce, glistening wet terrazzo specular reflections, and selective warm practical anchors (courtyard sodium lamp, faint green reception desk lamp, red `CẤP CỨU` emergency box sign glow, player flashlight).
2. **B (2005 Operational Report — `#50776C` uncanny institutional order):**
   - Clean, slightly too-perfect hospital teal dado (`#3E6659`), dry matte-ivory limewash (`#C4C5B0`), overhead 4800K greenish-white dual T8 fluorescent pools, spinning 3-blade ceiling fans, dry spotless terrazzo tiles, operational clock frozen at `03:17`.
   - B is **never** presented as objective historical truth; its sterility feels subtly administrative and eerie compared to R's physical permanence.
3. **M / Vignette (Bé An C01 Moment — `#D0C9A9` warm muted ivory halo):**
   - Gentle translucent apparition rim light without hostile glare or strobe; strictly obeys reduced-motion and accessibility settings.

## 4. Technical & Performance Targets (Preserved from M1-P)

- **Reference Machine & Target:** Intel Core Ultra 7 155H · Intel Arc iGPU · 16 GB RAM · Windows x64 D3D12 · **1080p Medium @ 60 Hz VSync (`P95 ≤ 16.67 ms`)**, Low & High presets preserved.
- **Asset Budgets:**
  - Static architecture & furniture: `LOD0 → LOD1 (48%) → LOD2 (22%)` + simplified box collision proxies on `SolidLayer (9)`.
  - Total active texture memory under Medium streaming budget: `≤ 256 MiB` (keep 2048² hero maps + 1024² secondary/character maps with mipmap streaming on tileables; evidence/signage kept unstreamed crisp).
  - Multi-scene Static Occlusion Culling baked across `Bootstrap + LOC01–LOC04` so cross-zone geometry is aggressively culled.
  - Zero `> 33 ms` R↔B transition spike during normal gameplay (resolved via prebuilt regional NavMesh snapshots, PSO warm-up, staggered reflection probes, and worker-thread durable flush during fade peak).
